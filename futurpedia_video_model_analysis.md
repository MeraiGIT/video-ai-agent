## 2. Overall Model Rankings

### The "Big Four" (Market Leaders)

1. **Kling 3.0 (Kuaishou):** The new overall champion. It balances high-fidelity physics, industry-leading lip-sync/emotion, and complex prompt adherence better than any single competitor.
2. **Veo 3.1 (Google):** The runner-up and specialist king. Unbeatable for Image-to-Video style retention and consistency, but slightly slower/less dynamic in action than Kling.
3. **Sora 2 (OpenAI):** The Text-to-Video physics engine. Incredible speed and motion for pure text prompts but falls apart in Image-to-Video and character consistency.
4. **Grok Imagine (xAI/Artlist):** The "Chaos" specialist. Surprisingly effective at handling weird, multi-subject prompts that break other models.

### The Solid Mid-Tier

5. **Wan 2.1 (Juan):** A very strong all-rounder. Rarely S-tier, but almost never fails completely. Great text tracking.
6. **Kling 2.6:** The previous standard. Still excellent for raw physics and action, but surpassed by 3.0 in fidelity and features.
7. **Hailuo (Hyo):** The "Physics Savant." Capable of random S-Tier moments involving cars or balls, but inconsistent elsewhere.

### The Niche / Low Tier

8. **Sea Dance:** Surprisingly good at emotion (crying) and fighting, but poor consistency otherwise.
9. **Runway Gen-3 Alpha/4.5:** Great for camera control (rack focus) and text titles, but struggles significantly with biological movement (humans/animals).
10. **LTX:** The bottom of the pack. Frequently fails complex prompts, though occasionally decent at camera moves.

## 3. Per-Model Detailed Analysis (All Models)

### 1. Kling 3.0 (Kuaishou)

- **Best At:** **Lip Sync & Emotion.** It delivers the most natural dialogue and authentic facial expressions (crying/laughing) of any model. Also S-tier for **Complex Action** sequences.
- **Good At:** Physics, long shots (up to 15s), and character consistency via "Omnireference."
- **Bad At:** **Text Generation.** It still struggles to spell words correctly compared to Grok or Runway.

### 2. Veo 3.1 (Google)

- **Best At:** **Style Retention.** If you upload an image, Veo preserves the artistic style (lighting, texture) better than anyone. Also excellent at Image-to-Video dialogue.
- **Good At:** General movement, rack focus, and smooth camera work.
- **Bad At:** **Speed & Physics.** Often defaults to "slow motion," making high-octane action feel sluggish.

### 3. Sora 2 (OpenAI)

- **Best At:** **Raw Physics (Text-to-Video).** Handles breakdancing, basketball arcs, and fast movement perfectly when prompted with text.
- **Good At:** Text rendering on objects (alarm clocks).
- **Bad At:** **Image-to-Video.** Frequently refuses prompts or hallucinates new styles, losing the original image's intent.

### 4. Grok Imagine (xAI)

- **Best At:** **Chaotic/Complex Prompts.** If you need a praying mantis walking past a cat in a pimp suit, Grok is the only one that gets it right. Great at managing multiple distinct characters.
- **Good At:** Robot precision, Title cards.
- **Bad At:** **Uncanny Valley.** Human faces can sometimes look slightly "off" or plastic compared to Kling/Veo.

### 5. Wan 2.1 ("Juan")

- **Best At:** **Object Tracking.** It was the only model to successfully write "Hello" on a chalkboard while tracking the hand correctly.
- **Good At:** General consistency. It rarely "fails" a prompt completely, usually landing in A or B tier.
- **Bad At:** **Audio/Lip Sync.** Lacks the advanced audio features of the top 4.

### 6. Kling 2.6

- **Best At:** **Action Physics.** Still a powerhouse for motorcycle chases and basketball shots.
- **Good At:** Crying/Emotion (was S-tier before 3.0).
- **Bad At:** **Text Generation.** Fails almost every text-related task (logos, signs).

### 7. Hailuo ("Hyo")

- **Best At:** **Vehicle Physics.** Surprisingly achieved S-Tier for the Car Chase sequence, handling collisions better than Veo.
- **Good At:** Basketball physics.
- **Bad At:** **Text & Consistency.** Often hallucinates or morphs characters oddly in longer shots.

### 8. Sea Dance

- **Best At:** **Specific Emotions.** Shockingly S-Tier for the "Crying Woman" prompt and very good at fight choreography.
- **Good At:** Camera focus effects.
- **Bad At:** **Morphing.** Characters tend to blend into the background or distort during complex motion.

### 9. Runway (Gen-3 Alpha/4.5)

- **Best At:** **Camera Control & Titles.** Excellent at "Rack Focus" and generating stable text for title screens.
- **Good At:** Landscapes and static scenes.
- **Bad At:** **Biological Motion.** Fails hard at breakdancing, fighting, or complex human movement (often D-Tier).

### 10. LTX

- **Best At:** **Camera moves.** Can handle a simple rack focus or pan.
- **Good At:** Occasional dialogue shots (A-Tier for Gladiator lip sync).
- **Bad At:** **Everything Else.** Frequently produces unusable video for physics, complex action, or text tasks.
